# ADR 0039: Registers Engine Extensions for Client-Subject CDD and High-Risk Opening Approval

- **Date:** 2026-11-24
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Roadmap:** Stage 1 decisions `S1-D5`, `S1-D7`, `S1-D10`; Work Package 2 slices `S1-04` and `S1-05` ([stage-1-plan.md](../roadmap/stage-1-plan.md)); backlog item `4`
- **Builds on:** [ADR 0014](./0014-staff-matter-access-by-permission.md) (staff-matter & restricted access), [ADR 0025](./0025-permission-enforcement-pattern.md) (permission enforcement), [ADR 0028](./0028-tenant-database-and-repositories.md) (tenant databases & repositories), [ADR 0030](./0030-tenant-aware-scheduler.md) (tenant-aware scheduler), [ADR 0032](./0032-registers-engine.md) (registers engine), [ADR 0038](./0038-intake-check-interfaces-and-manual-adapters.md) (intake check interfaces & `'evidence'` storage category)

## Context

[ADR 0032](./0032-registers-engine.md) established `core/src/domain/registers/` and `core/src/tenancy/registerRepository.ts` (`TenantRegisterRepository`) as the single configuration-driven engine for registers, approvals, and scheduled reminders (`sign_off`, `undertaking`, `complaint`, `breach`). Stage 1 Work Package 2 introduces two compliance workflows on top of that engine — **Client Due Diligence (`cdd` in `S1-04`, decision `S1-D5`)** and **High-Risk Matter Opening Approval (`high_risk_opening` in `S1-05`, decision `S1-D7`)** — which require generic extensions that do not exist in the engine today:

1. **Subject scoping beyond matters (`person_id` / `organisation_id`):**
   - `public.register_records` already declares nullable `person_id` and `organisation_id` columns (`core/src/tenancy/migrations/index.ts:2048-2051`), yet `0017_registers_engine_expand` only indexes `(tenant_id, register_type_key, status, updated_at DESC)`, `matter_id`, `due_at`, and `review_at` (`core/src/tenancy/migrations/index.ts:2068-2080`; `0018` adds GIN/trigram search indexes) — leaving `person_id` and `organisation_id` unindexed. `TenantRegisterRepository.listVisibleRegisterRecords` (`core/src/tenancy/registerRepository.ts:516-572`) and `listRegistersWorkspaceDataRecord` (`src/server/services.ts:27066-27190`) filter only by `matterId` and `enquiryId`. CDD lives on the **client** (`Person` or `Organisation`), reused across their matters while valid.
   - In addition, `REGISTER_RECORD_VISIBLE_SQL_PREDICATE` (`core/src/tenancy/registerRepository.ts:39-52`) diverges from `buildMatterHiddenFromViewerSql` (`core/src/tenancy/intakeQuoteRepository.ts:812-842`): it lacks the empty-allow-list participant fallback and `restrictedAccessUserIdsFallback`, and `listRegistersWorkspaceDataRecord` (`src/server/services.ts:27111-27117`) omits `hasRestrictedMatterAccess` when calling `listVisibleRegisterRecords`.
2. **Checklist and evidence fields:**
   - `RegisterFieldType` (`core/src/api/registers.types.ts:29-37`) supports scalar fields (`'text' | 'textarea' | 'number' | 'currency_gbp' | 'boolean' | 'date' | 'select' | 'person'`), but has no structured checklist item array or verified evidence-attachment field linked to the S1-03 `'evidence'` blob category and `public.intake_check_records`.
   - Furthermore, `/api/documents/download` (`src/app/api/documents/download/route.ts:43-86`) requires a `public.documents` row (`verified.documentId`) and only sets PDF/DOCX MIME types, so raw uploaded CDD evidence blobs (`tenant-<tenantId>-<env>/evidence/<objectId>/<fileName>`) cannot be downloaded through it. Meanwhile `IntakeEvidenceArtifactRef` (`core/src/api/index.ts:2166-2172`) currently holds only `{ blobKey, sha256, contentType, sizeBytes, scanStatus }`, `verifyEvidenceArtifacts` (`core/src/integrations/intake-checks/manualProviders.ts:81-133`) is module-private, and `putObject` (`core/src/tenancy/blobStorage.ts:214-231`) stores bytes and returns `scanStatus` without refusing infected files.
3. **Two-stage high-risk approval (`S1-D7`):**
   - `CORE_REGISTER_TYPES` (`core/src/domain/registers/index.ts:67-437`) supports only type-wide `approvalRules.allowedApproverRoles`, enforces no distinction between sequential approvers (checking only `ownerId` / `createdById` and optional `assignedApproverId`), and skips **only** the `allowedApproverRoles` role check for `'admin'` **only** on `transition.forbidSelfAction` transitions (`core/src/domain/registers/index.ts:1010-1023`, line `:1013` — never bypassing self-approval or `assignedApproverId` checks).
   - Owner decisions for Stage 1 require a High risk matter to be approved **first** by a Partner (`'partner'`, `'managing_partner'`), and **second** by a **different** person holding `'risk_compliance'`, with **zero admin bypass** unless that individual legitimately holds the required role and differs from the previous approver.

Because modifying `RegisterPermission`, `RegisterFieldType`, `RegisterTransitionDefinition`, `RegisterApprovalRules`, `IntakeEvidenceArtifactRef`, and core transition invariants changes public contracts in `@platform/core-api` (`core/src/api/registers.types.ts`, `core/src/api/index.ts`) and core engine semantics, this ADR is required before `S1-04` and `S1-05` implementation begins (`CLAUDE.md`).

## Decision

### 1. Client-subject registers (`subjectScope`), employee-client hiding, merge repointing & visibility convergence
- **Additive `subjectScope` on `RegisterTypeDefinition` (`core/src/api/registers.types.ts`):**
  - Add optional `subjectScope?: 'matter' | 'client' | 'any'` (default `'any'`, retaining `requireMatterLink?: boolean` unchanged).
  - Widen `RegisterPermission` additively with `'cdd:view' | 'cdd:record'` so core register type `'cdd'` binds `permissions` to `'cdd:view'` and `'cdd:record'` (preventing `admin` or `finance` from accessing CDD via generic `register:view` / `register:edit`).
  - Register types with `subjectScope: 'client'` (such as core `'cdd'`) enforce inside `validateRegisterCreateInput` and `TenantRegisterRepository.createRegisterRecord`:
    - exactly one of `personId` or `organisationId` must be set (and not both, unless explicitly configured otherwise), while `matterId` stays `null` (client CDD records belong to the client, not a single matter);
    - `TenantPartyRepository` (`getPersonById` / `getOrganisationById`) verifies inside `withTenantTransaction` that the target subject exists in the caller's tenant and is not merged (`mergedIntoId == null`), failing closed with `NOT_FOUND` on non-existent or cross-tenant IDs;
    - **Employee-client full hiding (`R747`, ADR 0014):** for subjects with `person.isEmployee === true` (or active/leaver employment records), the **entire** CDD record — CDD card, status text (never displaying `"CDD not started"`, `"Recorded manually on..."`, or compliance pills), `/registers?type=cdd` rows, `'evidence'` downloads, and re-check reminders — is visible **only** to users holding **`'cdd:view'` AND** (`'risk:view'` or `'matter:restricted:access'`, or active participation (`leadFeeEarnerId`, `responsiblePartnerId`, `supervisingPartnerId`, or `teamMemberIds`) in a matter for that employee-client). Because `DEFAULT_RBAC_ROLES.admin.permissions` (`core/src/domain/rbac/registry.ts:858-862`) explicitly filters out `'cdd:view'` and `'cdd:record'` alongside `'undertaking:manage'`, `admin` cannot inspect employee-client CDD even though `admin` holds `'risk:view'` and `'matter:restricted:access'`. All other staff see no CDD card or register row at all (since revealing CDD presence implies a staff matter).
  - **Immediate `'recorded'` completion & Standard → High upgrade (`R749` & Owner Answer 2):** CDD records transition directly into non-terminal completed state `'recorded'` once all required checklist items are recorded (no second approver sits on client CDD; two-person control sits on `high_risk_opening`). When a client holds an existing `'standard'` CDD record and an opening matter (or upgraded organisation risk) requires `'high'` tier, a transactional upgrade transition (`upgrade_to_high_risk`) appends the required High-risk items onto the existing record, returns its status to `'in_progress'` while preserving already-recorded Standard items, and recalculates the 1-year re-check interval once the upgraded checklist completes.
- **PostgreSQL indexes, backfill & merge repointing (`0025` + `executeMergeInTransaction`):**
  - Add partial indexes on `public.register_records`:
    - `idx_register_records_tenant_person ON public.register_records (tenant_id, person_id, register_type_key, status) WHERE person_id IS NOT NULL`
    - `idx_register_records_tenant_organisation ON public.register_records (tenant_id, organisation_id, register_type_key, status) WHERE organisation_id IS NOT NULL`
  - Backfill `organisation_type` `systemKey` values into `choice_values.metadata_json->>'systemKey'` following migration `0021`'s reversible `migration_0025_backfill_audit` + `jsonb_set` + `NOT EXISTS` pattern (`core/src/tenancy/migrations/index.ts:2735-3017`; `choice_values` has no `system_key` column).
  - Extend `ListVisibleRegisterRecordsOptions` (`core/src/tenancy/registerRepository.ts`) with optional `personId`, `personIds`, `organisationId`, and `organisationIds` filters, plus `viewerIsMatterParticipant` / employee-client SQL filtering.
  - Extend `executeMergeInTransaction` (`core/src/tenancy/partyRepository.ts:3713-3757`) in `S1-04` so merging people or organisations atomically repoints `public.intake_check_records.subject_id` and `public.evidence_artifacts.subject_id` (matching `subject_type` `'person' | 'organisation'`) alongside existing `register_records` (`person_id`, `organisation_id`, `field_values_json`) repointing (`R754`). Extend `updateRegisterRecordDetails` in `src/server/services.ts` only for explicit staff correction when `subjectScope` permits.
- **SQL matter-visibility convergence & barrier readiness (`S1-09` composition):**
  - Replace the duplicate SQL check in `REGISTER_RECORD_VISIBLE_SQL_PREDICATE` (`core/src/tenancy/registerRepository.ts:39-52`) with the shared `buildMatterHiddenFromViewerSql` helper (`core/src/tenancy/intakeQuoteRepository.ts:812-842`), and pass `hasRestrictedMatterAccess` in both `listRegistersWorkspaceDataRecord` (`src/server/services.ts:27111-27117`) and `listTransitionsForRecord` inside `getRegisterRecordDetailRecord` (`src/server/services.ts:27249-27251`). (Stage 1 §5 row `S1-09` records that `registerRepository` convergence is completed here in `S1-04`.)
  - Client-subject records (`matter_id IS NULL`) apply employee-client hiding (`R747`) in `S1-04`; once `S1-09` adds core information barrier tables (`information_barriers`, `information_barrier_targets`, `information_barrier_principals`), `buildClientRegisterVisibleSql` will compose cleanly via `NOT EXISTS (...)` on `person_id` / `organisation_id` / active barrier principals without schema rework.

### 2. Checklist and evidence field types, `'evidence'` artifact indexing & retention
- **Additive `RegisterFieldType` and `IntakeEvidenceArtifactRef` members (`core/src/api/registers.types.ts`, `core/src/api/index.ts`):**
  - Widen `RegisterFieldType` to include `'checklist' | 'evidence'`.
  - Extend `IntakeEvidenceArtifactRef` (`core/src/api/index.ts:2166-2172`) additively with optional metadata `{ fileName?: string; uploadedByActorId?: string; uploadedAt?: string }` alongside existing `{ blobKey, sha256, contentType, sizeBytes, scanStatus }` (with authoritative upload metadata also stored on `public.evidence_artifacts`).
  - **`'checklist'` field value contract:** stores a validated JSON array of checklist item entries (`RegisterChecklistItemValue[]`):
    - `itemKey: string` (matches configured template key from client type × risk tier, plus ad-hoc additional items if allowed);
    - `label: string`;
    - `required: boolean`;
    - `status: 'pending' | 'recorded' | 'not_applicable' | 'failed'`;
    - `recordedById: string | null`, `recordedByName?: string`, `recordedAt: string | null` (server-stamped ISO timestamp);
    - `note?: string` (trimmed free text; mandatory when `status === 'not_applicable'` or `'failed'`);
    - `evidenceRefs: IntakeEvidenceArtifactRef[]`;
    - `intakeCheckRecordId?: string | null` (links to `public.intake_check_records.id` `ICHK-*` when backed by S1-03 `IdentityVerificationProvider` or `SanctionsPepScreeningProvider`).
  - **`'evidence'` field value contract:** stores `IntakeEvidenceArtifactRef[]`.
- **Uploaded `'evidence'` artifact lifecycle (wiring S1-03 storage end to end):**
  - Introduce `public.evidence_artifacts` in expand migration `0025` (`(tenant_id, id)` PK `EVD-000001`, `blob_key`, `subject_type`, `subject_id`, `file_name`, `content_type`, `byte_size`, `sha256`, `scan_status`, `uploaded_by_person_id`, `uploaded_at`) with a partial index on `(tenant_id, subject_type, subject_id)`. (Default privileges from cluster bootstrap already cover new tables for `<runtimeRole>` — no explicit `GRANT` needed.)
  - Uploads accept size-capped files (configurable up to `10 MiB` default, allowed MIME/extensions: `.pdf`, `.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.webp`, `.txt`), **call exported `scanBytesForMalwareAndMacros` (`core/src/tenancy/blobStorage.ts:104`) BEFORE calling `putObject`**, and **refuse infected or macro-bearing files with newly defined error code `EVIDENCE_MALWARE_DETECTED`** (never persisting infected bytes to storage, since `putObject` itself stores bytes and returns `scanStatus`). Clean files are stored via `putObject` under `category: 'evidence'` (`tenant-<tenantId>-<env>/evidence/<objectId>/<safeName>`) and persisted to `public.evidence_artifacts` inside `withTenantTransaction`.
  - Export `verifyEvidenceArtifacts` (`core/src/integrations/intake-checks/manualProviders.ts:81-133`) in `S1-04` (preserving optional `{ fileName?, uploadedByActorId?, uploadedAt? }` fields). Because `verifyEvidenceArtifacts` performs blob-storage I/O (`getMalwareScanStatus`, `getObjectBytes`), run it **immediately before** `withTenantTransaction` (matching `ADR 0040` §3's no-I/O-inside-TX discipline), then verify `public.evidence_artifacts` DB ownership, checksum, and clean `scan_status` atomically **inside** `withTenantTransaction` when attaching an artifact to a register record.
  - Downloads use a dedicated `GET /api/evidence/download` route (`guardedRoute`, policy `{ kind: 'permission', key: 'cdd:view', enforcedBy: 'action' }` — requiring `'cdd:view'` strictly rather than `'register:view'` or `'risk:view'`, verified via `verifyScopedDownloadToken`, database lookup in `public.evidence_artifacts`, and `R747` employee-client subject access, returning `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` and `Content-Disposition: attachment`). `/api/documents/download` (`src/app/api/documents/download/route.ts`) stays untouched for `public.documents` generated templates/files.
  - **Retention boundary (`R758`):** `cdd` register records, `public.intake_check_records`, `public.evidence_artifacts`, and `'evidence'` blobs are **never** deleted or scrubbed by any existing retention or anonymisation job (`enquiry-retention-anonymise`, `usage-retention-purge`, `outbox-drain`). Automated regulatory retention (retain for 5 years after the client relationship ends, then delete) is logged as a pre-hosted-client backlog item (`S1-04` §11).
  - Stage 0 Decision D4 and Risk R3 remain unchanged: `local-fs` is restricted to single-replica non-live demos and fails closed in `'live'` (`STORAGE_LIVE_LOCAL_FS_FORBIDDEN`) until `azure-blob` is implemented before the first hosted client.

### 3. Per-transition `allowedRoles`
- Add optional `allowedRoles?: readonly HomeRoleKey[]` to `RegisterTransitionDefinition` (`core/src/api/registers.types.ts`, mirrored in `core/schemas/registers.config.schema.json` and Zod `core/src/config-engine/index.ts`).
- Precedence inside `validateRegisterTransitionExecution` (`core/src/domain/registers/index.ts`):
  1. When `transition.allowedRoles` is defined and non-empty, the actor's resolved authoritative `HomeRoleKey` set (`actorRoles`) must intersect `transition.allowedRoles`.
  2. When omitted or empty on a transition governed by `approvalRules`, existing `registerType.approvalRules.allowedApproverRoles` applies unchanged.
  3. Values must be valid built-in or tenant-configured role keys (never display labels).

### 4. Distinct-from-previous-approver rule (`mustDifferFromPreviousApprover`) and `'withdrawn'` lifecycle (`R752`)
- Add optional fields to `RegisterTransitionDefinition`:
  - `mustDifferFromPreviousApprover?: boolean` (default `false`);
  - `distinctFromTransitionKeys?: readonly string[]` (optional explicit transition keys whose prior actors in the active approval cycle must differ from the current actor; when omitted and `mustDifferFromPreviousApprover === true`, compares against all prior approval-category / `forbidSelfAction` transitions in the active cycle).
- **Active approval cycle boundary:** All transitions on `register_record_id` occurring **strictly after** the most recent `'reopen'`, `'resubmit'`, `'request_changes'`, `'withdrawn'`, or reset-to-initial transition (or since record creation if none). Thus, if a High-risk matter is returned to drafting (`'return'` / `'reject'` then resubmitted), or re-scored down (`withdrawn`) and later re-scored back up, a fresh approval cycle begins cleanly; within the same cycle, the Risk & Compliance approver cannot be the Partner who executed `partner_approve` — even if one person holds both `partner` and `risk_compliance` roles!
- **Atomic in-transaction enforcement:** `TenantRegisterRepository.lockAndExecuteTransition` acquires `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` on `public.register_records`, reads the ordered rows from `public.register_transitions WHERE tenant_id = $1 AND register_record_id = $2 ORDER BY transitioned_at ASC, id ASC`, and passes prior cycle actor IDs into `validateRegisterTransitionExecution`. Matching any disallowed prior approver fails closed with newly defined stable error code **`REGISTER_DISTINCT_APPROVER_REQUIRED`**.
- Existing `forbidSelfAction` (`actorId === record.ownerId || actorId === record.createdById` → `REGISTER_SELF_APPROVAL_FORBIDDEN`) continues to apply independently so neither the creator/owner nor the prior approver can self-approve or double-hat both steps.
- **Automatic withdrawal of stale high-risk approvals (`R752`):** Add terminal state `'withdrawn'` (`category: 'cancelled'` / closed, label `"Withdrawn"`) and internal system transition `withdraw_opening` (`['pending_partner', 'pending_compliance'] -> 'withdrawn'`) on `'high_risk_opening'`. When a prospective matter's server-derived rating drops below High/Critical before opening, or when the prospective matter is deleted or abandoned, the server automatically transitions any pending `'high_risk_opening'` record to `'withdrawn'` inside the same transaction (audited under the system actor / action correlation), removing it from partner and compliance Home queues. Re-scoring back up later creates a fresh `'pending_partner'` approval cycle. An already-`'approved'` record is **never** withdrawn.

### 5. Explicit `allowAdminBypass` control & error codes (`R756`, eliminating admin bypass on `high_risk_opening`)
- Add optional `allowAdminBypass?: boolean` to `RegisterApprovalRules` (`core/src/api/registers.types.ts`, default `true` when omitted).
- Inside `validateRegisterTransitionExecution` (`core/src/domain/registers/index.ts:1010-1023`):
  - Today's `!actorRoles.includes('admin')` check (`:1013`) skips **only** the `allowedApproverRoles` role check, and **only** on `transition.forbidSelfAction` transitions (it never skips self-approval or `assignedApproverId` checks). Replace that hardcoded check with `!(registerType.approvalRules.allowAdminBypass !== false && actorRoles.includes('admin'))`.
  - For existing core types (`sign_off`, `breach`), `allowAdminBypass` remains omitted/`true`, preserving S0-24 broken-glass semantics verbatim.
  - For **`high_risk_opening`** (confirmed by the owner on 24 November 2026), core sets **`allowAdminBypass: false`**, and **`mergeRegistersConfigurations`** marks `allowAdminBypass: false`, transitions, `allowedRoles`, and `mustDifferFromPreviousApprover` on `high_risk_opening` as **core-locked invariants** that tenant `typeOverrides` or Admin UI configuration cannot weaken. An administrator who does not genuinely hold the transition's `allowedRoles` is refused with existing error code **`REGISTER_APPROVER_MISMATCH`** (`core/src/domain/registers/index.ts:31`), and even a dual-role admin cannot bypass `forbidSelfAction`, `mustDifferFromPreviousApprover`, or `distinctFromTransitionKeys`.

### 6. Backward compatibility (Rule 7) and `@platform/core-api` versioning
- Every type, field, and permission addition (`subjectScope`, `RegisterPermission` additions `'cdd:view' | 'cdd:record'`, `'checklist'`, `'evidence'`, additive optional `IntakeEvidenceArtifactRef` fields `fileName` / `uploadedByActorId` / `uploadedAt`, `allowedRoles`, `mustDifferFromPreviousApprover`, `distinctFromTransitionKeys`, `allowAdminBypass`, `'withdrawn'` state support, `RegisterChecklistItemValue`, and `EvidenceArtifactRecord`) is strictly **additive and optional** on existing structures.
- New error codes explicitly defined by this ADR: **`REGISTER_DISTINCT_APPROVER_REQUIRED`** (registers domain) and **`EVIDENCE_MALWARE_DETECTED`** (evidence upload service). Existing role-mismatch error handling reuses **`REGISTER_APPROVER_MISMATCH`** (`core/src/domain/registers/index.ts:31`).
- Existing `sign_off`, `undertaking`, `complaint`, `breach`, and any tenant-defined register configurations stored in `client-<slug>/config/` or `public.tenant_module_configs['registers']` validate and execute identically with zero breaking schema or runtime changes.
- Raise `CORE_API_VERSION` from `'2.1.0'` to **`'2.2.0'`** (additive minor bump within `2.x`); existing extension manifests declaring `^2.0.0` remain valid without modification.

### 7. Behaviour across both hosting models (Rule 6)
- **Model A (`single_tenant`):** A single dedicated database and single tenant container store `register_records`, `register_transitions`, `evidence_artifacts`, and `evidence/` blobs; all queries and checks still require `VerifiedOidcTokenClaims`-derived `TenantContext` (`SET LOCAL ROLE`), and `local-fs` remains demo-only until `azure-blob` lands before live client onboarding.
- **Model B (`multi_tenant`):** Each tenant has its own isolated PostgreSQL database (`pg_tenant_<slug>_<env>`) and its own container (`tenant-<tenantId>-<env>/evidence/...`). Every register query, transition lock, download token signature (`tokenSigningKey:tenantId`), audit entry, and reminder job includes and asserts `tenantId` (`assertRowTenant`, `assertBlobKeyBelongsToTenant`), with zero cross-tenant leakage verified by two-tenant tests (`tenant-alpha` vs `tenant-bravo`).

## Alternatives Considered

1. **Create bespoke PostgreSQL tables (`client_cdd_records`, `high_risk_approvals`) outside the registers engine:** Rejected by decisions `S1-D5` and `S1-D7` and ADR 0032. Duplicating state machines, `row_version` locking, audit entries, reminder states, and extension hooks creates parallel maintenance and isolation risk.
2. **Allow `'admin'` bypass everywhere via existing `core/src/domain/registers/index.ts:1013` logic:** Rejected explicitly by owner decision (24 November 2026) and `S1-D7`; high-risk regulatory sign-off requires independent partner + Risk & Compliance approval that `admin` cannot override.
3. **Remove `admin` bypass retroactively from `sign_off` and `breach`:** Rejected under Rule 7 (backward compatibility) and existing S0-24 contract tests; gating opt-out via `allowAdminBypass: false` preserves S0-24 guarantees while locking `high_risk_opening`.
4. **Serve CDD evidence through `/api/documents/download` by inserting fake `public.documents` rows:** Rejected because generated documents (`documents`, templates, SharePoint links) have distinct schemas and access rules (`canActorAccessGeneratedDocument`) from manual verification evidence stored in `evidence/` blobs and `intake_check_records`.

## Consequences

- `S1-04` (`cdd` register type) and `S1-05` (`high_risk_opening` register type) reuse the hardened `TenantRegisterRepository`, `register_transitions` history, `row_version` optimistic concurrency, extension hooks, and `register-reminders` scheduler without building a second workflow engine or Bicep job.
- Requires reversible expand migration `0025_registers_cdd_and_org_type_keys_expand` (`CORE_REQUIRED_SCHEMA_VERSION = '0025'`), `POST /api/evidence/upload`, `GET /api/evidence/download`, merge repointing of `intake_check_records` & `evidence_artifacts`, and two-tenant PostgreSQL + tampered-ID tests covering client-subject registers, full employee-client hiding, evidence checksum/malware/tenant checks, sequential distinct approvers, automatic `'withdrawn'` on down-scoring, and refusal of `admin` bypass on `high_risk_opening`.
- Existing Stage 1 Risk `R3` remains unchanged: **no live tenant may store real CDD evidence until `azure-blob` replaces `local-fs`.**
